Milestone 6 acceptance: static scene

Purpose
-------
M6 replaces the legacy presentation tour with one explicitly named,
host-owned STATIC_SCHWARZSCHILD scene. The body layout and disk look do not
tour, breathe, drift, or reset. A bounded deterministic disk-material layer
may evolve only at traced intersections with the analytic slim disk body.

Fixed approved scene
--------------------
- center: (0.50, 0.50)
- apparent radius: 0.120
- temperature: 5500 K
- inclination: 1.50 rad
- roll: 0.35 rad
- disk radii: 1.80 to 8.00
- baseline opacity / Doppler: 0.90 / 0.60
- beam / gain / contrast / wind / material speed / exposure:
  2.50 / 2.20 / 1.60 / 7.00 / 5.00 / 1.40

Preserved behavior
------------------
- OpenGL 3.3, one fullscreen draw, fixed N_STEPS 48, and one-frame GPU-fence
  scheduling. Slow frames receive only a bounded one-quarter cooldown after
  retirement, so the safety policy does not double visible sky-motion gaps.
- M3 directional sky and local source-plane lensing remain unchanged. Every
  deflected star path may gather adjacent procedural catalogue cells only when
  its source coordinate is near a catalogue-cell edge; direct-sky stars keep
  their original one-cell point-sprite path. This bounded seam treatment
  prevents an entry/exit flash without deliberately blurring or dimming a star.
  Only non-captured, truly near-tangential exits use a bounded source-plane
  projection and smoothly return to direct sky; stable escaping rays retain
  their full lens deflection instead of switching at a binary exit gate.
- M4 versioned /c configuration behavior and its star brightness, disk
  opacity, and Doppler mapping remain unchanged.
- uSkySeed remains a sky-layout and sky-flow seed. uSceneSeed does not affect
  position, size, inclination, roll, or look in M6; it seeds only the bounded
  disk-space impact-arc material events.
- M6 requires visible relative motion: the black-hole composition is fixed
  while the directional background sky translates at `SKY_FLOW_SPEED = 0.0750`
  (50% faster than the prior M6 calibration). `uSkySeed` selects one random
  direction once per screensaver launch; that direction remains straight and
  fixed during the run. It must not rotate around the black-hole or screen
  center. Within the strong-lensing ring, a parity-
  reversed secondary image may move locally opposite the direct background;
  that qualitative lens behavior is allowed, but one-frame flashes and
  discontinuous teleports are not. A frame in which both the body composition
  and the background remain fixed is not an acceptable M6 result.

Disk material and geometry
--------------------------
- The disk is a finite analytic body with a constant half-thickness of
  `0.035`. It has upper/lower height faces and an outer rim; it is neither an
  infinitely thin plane nor a volume ray march.
- Each existing geodesic chord performs one bounded analytic face/rim query;
  it does not subdivide or consume the fixed 48-step geodesic budget. A disk
  contribution occurs only when a ray enters that body from outside. The paired
  exit does not double emission or opacity, while a later genuine re-entry
  remains valid. All local material quantities use the contact's normal-
  projected disk-space coordinate.
- The disk keeps its continuous wrapped-filament background and adds exactly
  three candidate analytic impact arcs. Each descriptor is reconstructed from
  time and uSceneSeed, so it persists coherently without a texture, particle
  buffer, fluid state, framebuffer, pass, or user setting.
- An arc begins as a short local disk-space ellipse at a seeded birth radius
  and azimuth. It then drifts inward exponentially while its birth-coordinate
  radius receives a Kepler-like phase; neighboring radii therefore shear into
  a curved tail rather than rotating as a rigid screen-space decal.
- Candidate slots are staggered every 11 seconds, with a per-event birth
  offset and lifetime of 18 to 22 seconds. Only that event fades; the disk has
  no 36-second global macro replay, synchronized fade, or whole-disk reset.
- The dark region below the truncated emissive inner edge is a non-emissive,
  smoothly opaque plunging region at a slim-disk-body entry. A separate smooth
  ray-space inner-flow silhouette blocks background-only rays that never enter
  the slim disk body. Together they keep stars out of the black gap between
  the photon ring and visible disk without reducing accumulated disk emission;
  the event-horizon shadow remains separate.
- The existing derivative-free footprint estimate attenuates an unresolved
  impact arc at the critical lens ring while leaving the baseline disk intact.
  This remains procedural disk material, not a particle system, fluid
  simulation, texture, asset, FBO, or added pass.

Explicitly deferred
-------------------

- Kerr LUT validation/integration and binary black-hole work.

Manual final M6 gate
--------------------
1. Run blackhole.scr /s and observe at least 45 seconds of the fixed composition and sky motion.
2. Confirm the black-hole center, apparent radius, disk inclination, roll, and
   overall disk look remain fixed, while direct-sky stars or clusters translate
   past that fixed body without orbiting it. On a subsequent launch, verify a
   random direction once per screensaver launch is selected; within each run
   the sky trajectory remains straight. Near the lens ring, a smooth
   locally reversed secondary image is allowed; stars retain their original
   apparent size and must not disappear at a procedural cell edge, show an
   entry/exit flash at the lens boundary, flash for one frame, abruptly turn
   black at a near-tangential exit, teleport, visibly pass through the black
   inner disk circle, or move normally in the black gap between photon ring and
   visible disk.
3. Confirm the disk has only a slight stable vertical rim/upper-lower extent,
   including at its outer edge, without becoming a broad torus or displaying
   two identical bright layers from its entry and exit boundaries.
4. Confirm one to three bright short arcs persist at a disk radius, orbit with
   visibly radius-dependent shear, drift inward, and fade independently. New
   arcs must not cause a disk-wide blink, 36-second reset, or common fade; they
   must remain disk-space structures across the lensed primary and secondary
   images rather than screen-space overlays.
5. Confirm the disk retains the black inner cavity and photon-ring-to-disk gap
   without background stars leaking through.
6. Run blackhole.scr /p in a Screen Saver Settings preview and confirm the
   same stable composition and deterministic material events are used.
7. Reply "M6 through" if acceptable, or report the visible motion and time at
   which it occurs.
